✨ Welcome to VibeFocus ✨

What is it? A cinematic focus timer designed to protect your attention in a distracted world.

The Goal: To help you enter the flow state instantly. By anchoring your vision to perpetual, calming loops, we lower your heart rate and eliminate the urge to context-switch.

How to use it:

  • 🎨
    1. Select an Atmosphere

    Choose a cinematic environment. Each scene is crafted to lower your heart rate and anchor your thoughts.

  • ⏱️
    2. Define the Boundary

    Allocate your attention. A 25-minute sprint is ideal for starting, while 90 minutes unlocks extreme depth.

  • 🧘
    3. Enter the Flow State

    Hit start, leave the noise behind, and let the perpetual motion keep you grounded in the present.

Ready to protect your presence? Close this and click Start Focus 🚀
