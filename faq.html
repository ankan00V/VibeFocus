Questions & Answers

Everything you need to know about VibeFocus, how it works, and how to get the most out of your deep work sessions.

❓

What makes this different?

VibeFocus replaces the traditional ticking clock with calming, cinematic visuals like a burning candle or filling water bowl, designed to anchor your attention for deep work without causing anxiety.

🧠

Is it ADHD-friendly?

Yes, VibeFocus is an excellent ADHD-friendly focus app. The continuous, subtle visual motion gives the brain just enough gentle stimulation to prevent distraction.

⏱️

How do I use it?

Simply select a calm atmosphere, choose your custom time blocking duration, and enter the flow state. The mindfulness timer will visually represent time passing seamlessly on your screen.

💻

Do I need an account?

No. VibeFocus is completely free to use online in your browser with zero sign-ups required. We believe deep work should be accessible immediately.
