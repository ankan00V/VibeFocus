About VibeFocus

The best free aesthetic Pomodoro timer for deep work and mindfulness online.

Why choose a visual focus timer for studying and deep work?

In a highly distracted world, traditional ticking clocks and loud alarms create anxiety. VibeFocus is a free, cinematic, visual focus app designed to protect your attention and boost your productivity. By anchoring your vision to perpetual, calming loops like a burning candle or a quiet forest, we lower your heart rate and eliminate the urge to context-switch, making it the perfect calm study timer for students and professionals.
