✨ About VibeFocus ✨

Our Mission: To protect your attention in a highly distracted world.

The Problem: Traditional ticking clocks and loud alarms create anxiety. The modern world is built to steal your attention, making deep work feel impossible.

The VibeFocus Approach:

  • 🔥
    Cinematic Anchors

    By anchoring your vision to perpetual, calming loops like a burning candle or a quiet forest, we lower your heart rate and eliminate the urge to context-switch.

  • 🧠
    ADHD-Friendly Design

    The continuous, subtle visual motion gives the brain just enough gentle stimulation to prevent distraction, acting as the perfect calm study timer.

  • 💸
    100% Free Forever

    No subscriptions, no paywalls. We believe the ability to focus and enter the flow state should be accessible to everyone.
