About VibeFocus

Traditional ticking clocks and loud alarms create anxiety. The modern world is built to steal your attention, making deep work feel impossible. We built VibeFocus to protect your peace.

🔥

Cinematic Anchors

By anchoring your vision to perpetual, calming loops like a burning candle or a quiet forest, we lower your heart rate and eliminate the urge to context-switch.

🧠

ADHD-Friendly Design

The continuous, subtle visual motion gives the brain just enough gentle stimulation to prevent distraction, acting as the perfect calm study timer.

💸

100% Free Forever

No subscriptions, no paywalls. We believe the ability to focus and enter the flow state should be accessible to everyone, everywhere.
